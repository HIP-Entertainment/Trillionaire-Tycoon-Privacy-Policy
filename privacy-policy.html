Privacy Policy for Trillionaire Tycoon

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Trillionaire Tycoon handles, both the Android app (package name com.trillionairetycoon.game, "the app") and the version you play in a web browser ("the web version"). Together they are "the game". The game is published by HIP Entertainment ("we", "us"). Contact: hipentr@gmail.com

By using the game you accept this policy. If you do not agree with it, please uninstall the app or stop using the web version.

1. The short version

  • There are no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of ours. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or precise location.
  • Your progress, settings and optional profile photo are stored only on your device (or in your browser, for the web version).
  • The Android app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect certain device information to show and measure ads (see section 4). We do not receive that information.
  • The Android app uses Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics) to see how the game is played and to find crashes (see section 5). You can turn this off at any time with Usage & crash data in the Profile tab.
  • The web version shows no ads and sends no analytics or crash reports (see section 7).
  • We do not sell personal data and we do not follow you across apps or devices.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (money, buildings, settings, a profile name and an optional profile photo you pick yourself) in local storage on your device: in the app's storage on your phone, or in your browser's local storage for the web version. We use it only to run the game, for example to restore your progress when you reopen it. We cannot see or access this data.

  • Save files: the Profile tab lets you export and import a save file. The file goes wherever you choose to put it, and we never receive it.
  • How long it is kept: until you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings (or clear your browser's site data, for the web version). The app is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup, so your progress is not copied to Google Drive or to a new phone. Use the export option to move it yourself.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect or receive: your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or files (the profile name, profile photo and save files stay on your device), payment details, or an account. The game has no in-app purchases and no accounts. The game sends no game data to any server of ours. Its network traffic is loading the game's fonts from Google Fonts (section 6), and, in the Android app only, ad requests to Google (section 4) and usage and crash reports to Google Firebase (section 5).

4. Ads and information collected by Google (Android app)

The Android app uses Google AdMob to show a banner ad at the bottom of the screen and optional rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect and use, for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention:

  • the Android advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • approximate location (derived from your IP address)
  • app interactions and ad interactions
  • diagnostic and crash information

This data is collected by Google, not by us. We do not receive it, and we cannot link it to you. Ads may come from Google and its advertising partners and technology providers. Google's practices are described at policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

The game is meant for a general audience (13 and older, see section 11). We limit ad content to a maximum rating of PG.

Your choices

  • In the EEA, the UK and other regions where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are shown. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with Ad privacy options in the Profile tab, when it is shown.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Watching a rewarded ad is always optional.

5. Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase, Android app)

In the Android app we use Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, services provided by Google, to understand how the game is played (for example, where players get stuck, so we can balance it) and to fix crashes. They collect:

  • an app instance ID: a random ID for this installation of the game, which changes when you reinstall the app
  • game events: starting a session, how long you play, buying a home or business for the first time, upgrades, permits, contracts, reaching goals, going public (IPO), claiming the daily reward, watching a rewarded ad, and which tabs of the game you open
  • device model, Android version, app version, language, and approximate country or region derived from your IP address
  • crash reports and error details: what the app was doing when it crashed or ran into an error

We do not send your profile name, profile photo or save file to Firebase. We do not link Firebase data to your advertising ID, and we do not use it for ads. We see analytics data mostly as totals and use it only to improve the game. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days. Google processes this data on our behalf; see firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Your choices

  • Usage & crash data in the Profile tab turns analytics and crash reporting off (and back on) at any time. When you turn it off, crash reports that were not sent yet are deleted from your device.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, analytics and crash reporting stay off until you agree to measurement in Google's consent form. You can change that choice with Ad privacy options in the Profile tab.

6. Permissions and network use

The Android app asks only for the permissions it needs: internet access and network state (to load fonts and ads and to send analytics and crash reports), and the advertising ID permission (so AdMob can read your advertising ID; Firebase does not use it). The app does not ask for access to your contacts, microphone, camera, location or storage. All connections use HTTPS.

Both the app and the web version load the game's fonts from Google Fonts. To deliver them, Google receives your IP address and basic browser information, as it would for any website that uses Google Fonts. See developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy.

7. The web version

  • No ads, analytics or crash reports: the web version does not include Google AdMob or Google Firebase, and it does not send game events or crash reports anywhere.
  • No cookies: the game sets no cookies and uses no tracking pixels. It uses your browser's local storage only to save your progress and settings (section 2).
  • Hosting: the website that serves the game may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of your visit, to deliver and protect the site.
  • Stopping: you can stop all of this at any time by closing the game. Clearing your browser's site data removes your saved progress.

8. Other information

  • Bug reports: the "report a bug" button only opens your own email app or webmail with a pre-filled message. Nothing is sent unless you send it. If you do, the message reaches us as an ordinary email and we use it only to fix the problem and reply to you.
  • Links: links such as our Discord invite open outside the game. Those services have their own privacy policies and we are not responsible for them.

9. Sharing, selling, security and breaches

We do not sell personal data. Ad data is handled by Google as described in section 4, and analytics and crash data by Google Firebase as described in section 5. We have no servers or databases of our own, so there is no database of ours to be breached. On your device, your progress is protected by your device's own security (for example the screen lock and Android's app sandbox). No method of storage is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Data breach notification: if a data breach occurs that affects your personal data, we will notify you in accordance with applicable legal requirements, including, where required, information about the nature of the breach and the steps being taken to address it.

10. Where data is processed

Google may process ad, analytics, crash and font data in the United States and other countries outside your country of residence, including outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Data protection laws in those countries may differ from those where you live. Where the law requires safeguards for international transfers, these rely on:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission
  • adequacy decisions or other legally recognised transfer mechanisms

Google's safeguards for international transfers are described in Google's privacy policy linked above. We process no personal data on servers of our own.

11. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us at the address below and we will help, for example by explaining how to remove the game's data from the device and deleting its analytics data.

12. Your rights and deleting data

  • Data on your device: you can remove it at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage (or clearing your browser's site data, for the web version).
  • Analytics and crash data: turn off Usage & crash data in the Profile tab to stop it. To have data that was already collected deleted, email us the ID shown under that setting (it is shown while the setting is on) and we will ask Google to delete it. Step-by-step instructions, and what is deleted or kept, are on our Delete Your Data page.
  • Data held by us: none other than the above. If you emailed us a bug report, you can ask us to delete that email.
  • Data held by Google for ads (for example your advertising ID): use Google's controls at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy or your Android ad settings.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. For analytics and crash data, contact us. For Google's ad processing, where Google is the controller, use Ad privacy options in the app or Google's tools above.
  • California and other US states: we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context advertising. For ad personalisation controlled by Google, use Ad privacy options in the app (when shown) or your Android ad settings. You will not be treated differently for using these choices.

You can also contact us at hipentr@gmail.com with any privacy request or question.
