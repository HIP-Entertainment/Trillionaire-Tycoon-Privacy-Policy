Delete Your Data: Trillionaire Tycoon

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This page explains how to delete your data from the game Trillionaire Tycoon (Android package com.trillionairetycoon.game), published by HIP Entertainment. Contact: hipentr@gmail.com

  • The game has no accounts and no sign-in, so there is no account to delete.
  • The only data we can have deleted for you is the usage and crash data sent to Google Firebase by the Android app. Follow the steps below.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device. You delete it yourself (see "Data on your device").

How to request deletion of your usage and crash data

  1. Open Trillionaire Tycoon on your Android phone and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Find Usage & crash data. Make sure it is On: the ID of your installation is shown under it only while it is on. Write the ID down or copy it.
  3. Send an email to hipentr@gmail.com with the subject Delete my data - Trillionaire Tycoon and the ID in the message.
  4. If you also want collection to stop, turn Usage & crash data Off after you have copied the ID.

We send your deletion request to Google within 30 days of receiving your email and reply to confirm. Google removes the data from reports within a few days and from its systems within about two months.

The ID changes when you reinstall the app. If you already uninstalled or reinstalled the game, we cannot find the old data, and it is deleted automatically when its retention period ends (see the table below).

Data on your device

Your progress, settings, profile name and profile photo never leave your device, so we cannot delete them for you. You can delete them at any time:

  • Reset in the Profile tab erases your progress in the game.
  • Uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings > Apps > Trillionaire Tycoon > Storage > Clear storage, to remove everything the game stored.
  • For the web version, clear your browser's site data for the game's website.

Deleting this data is permanent. Use Export in the Profile tab first if you want to keep a copy of your progress.

Ad data held by Google

Ads in the Android app are shown by Google AdMob. Google collects that data, not us, and we cannot delete it. You can:

  • delete or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads
  • use Google's controls at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy
  • change your ad choices with Ad privacy options in the Profile tab, when it is shown
